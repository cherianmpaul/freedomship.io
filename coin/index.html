Why FSC

FSC is not another crypto.

Most crypto projects exist to enrich early holders. FSC exists to build a fleet of ocean ships where 5,000 people per ship live completely free for life. Every design choice points at that mission — and away from speculation.

🪙

Fixed Supply, Forever

One billion FSC were minted on day one. The protocol cannot mint another coin — not for the team, not for marketing, not ever. No inflation can dilute what supporters have already given.

✓FSC: 1,000,000,000 — hard cap in the protocol.
✗Others: ongoing emissions, team allocations, hidden printers.
⚓

Backed by Real Ships

FSC isn't backed by a whitepaper. It's backed by steel, engines, kitchens, hospitals — physical ocean vessels carrying real families. Every coin sold pays for a real piece of the fleet.

✓FSC: 100% of sales fund ship construction & operations.
✗Others: "utility coming soon," runway burned on payroll.
🌿

No Mining. No Gas.

FSC runs on Holochain — an agent-centric peer network, not a global blockchain. No proof-of-work, no validator auction, no transaction gas fee. Sending FSC costs nothing and consumes almost no energy.

✓FSC: fee-free transfers, near-zero energy, no miners.
✗Others: gas spikes, PoW emissions, validator extraction.
🎁

Donation-Only Distribution

FSC isn't sold in an ICO. There is no presale, no airdrop, no VC tranche, no team unlock cliff. The only way to receive FSC is to donate to the Freedom Ship Trust through the FreedomShip app.

✓FSC: donate → receive. Every coin earned by giving.
✗Others: founder bags, exchange listings, paper-hand rugs.
🔐

You Own Your Coins

Your FSC lives on your device, on your own signed chain. No custodian holds your keys. No exchange can freeze you. Lose a device? Designated guardians help you recover — no central support line, no secret phrases to memorise.

✓FSC: self-sovereign keys, social recovery via friends.
✗Others: exchange custody, KYC freezes, 24-word phrases.
💌

A Coin of Belief

FSC is not an investment. It is a public, visible token of support for a mission you believe in — like a wristband for a cause, but cryptographic. The reward isn't speculation. The reward is the ships you helped build sailing the ocean carrying free families.

✓FSC: support instrument, not a security.
✗Others: "number-go-up" marketing, lambo memes.

The most important sentence on this page

FSC gives you zero priority for a cabin.

Cabins on every FreedomShip vessel are allocated strictly first-come, first-served from the public waiting list. Buying FSC does not move you up the line. It does not reserve a cabin. If you want a cabin, join the free waiting list. If you also believe in this mission, donate. They are two different acts — and we ask you to do both.

How It Works

Four steps. That's it.

The whole flow lives inside the FreedomShip app. No web wallets, no exchanges, no copy-pasting seed phrases. Just install, register, donate, hold.

01

Download the App

Get the FreedomShip app for your platform — desktop, mobile, or both. Same app, same identity, fully decentralised. Available below.

02

Create Your Identity

Your keys are generated on your device and protected by your phone's or laptop's biometric. No password, no recovery phrase to remember. Trusted friends (guardians) help if you ever lose every device.

03

Donate to the Trust

Inside the app, pick an amount and donate to the Freedom Ship Trust. Pay with card, bank transfer, or supported crypto. The Trust routes 100% to FreedomShip Inc. to build and operate the fleet — no founder is paid from it.

04

Receive FSC

Your share of FSC lands in your in-app wallet, signed onto your personal chain. Hold it, send it to friends, or just keep it as a quiet sign of what you helped build.

Built on Holochain

Decentralised. By design.

Holochain is fundamentally different from blockchains. There is no single global ledger to mine or stake. Each person — each agent — carries their own cryptographically signed chain of actions, validated peer-to-peer by the network. The result: FSC transfers are fee-free, near-instant, and don't require boiling a small lake to confirm.

⚡

Fee-Free Transfers

No gas. No "network fees." Sending FSC to a friend costs nothing and arrives in seconds.

🌱

Near-Zero Energy

No proof-of-work. No mining farms. The network runs on people's normal devices.

🛡️

Your Keys, Your Coins

Keys generated and stored on your device, protected by biometrics. No exchange custody.

🧭

No Central Authority

No company can shut down the network. The FSC ledger lives across the community itself.

FSC Questions

Everything you need to know.

Is FSC a cryptocurrency I can trade on exchanges?
+
FSC is a community-support coin, not a tradeable speculative asset. There is no official exchange listing, no token sale, and no roadmap to one. FSC exists to be donated for, held, sent between community members, and to represent your contribution to the fleet. Treat it as belief made visible, not as an investment.
Will buying more FSC get me a cabin faster?
+
No — and we will never let this change. Cabin allocation is strict first-come, first-served from the public waiting list, regardless of FSC holdings. Someone who joined the waiting list early and has never held a single FSC will be ahead of someone who joined later and donated a fortune. If you want a cabin, the action is to join the free waiting list. If you believe in the mission, the action is to donate. They are separate, and we keep them separate so the system stays fair.
How is FSC different from Bitcoin or Ethereum?
+
Three big ways. First, FSC has a hard one-billion fixed supply with zero ability to mint more — Bitcoin's supply still inflates until 2140; Ethereum has no hard cap. Second, FSC runs on Holochain (no global ledger, no mining, no gas fees) — sending FSC consumes essentially no energy. Third, FSC is distributed only via donation and is explicitly not an investment; the others are widely traded as speculative assets.
Why donation-only? Why not an ICO or airdrop?
+
Because the mission isn't to enrich early holders — it's to build ships. An ICO would funnel money to founders and venture funds. An airdrop would put coins in the hands of people who didn't earn them by supporting the project. Donating to the Freedom Ship Trust means every coin in circulation traces back to someone who consciously gave money to make this real.
What happens to my FSC if I lose my phone or laptop?
+
The app uses two recovery paths and never asks you to memorise a secret phrase. First: if you have a second linked device (your phone and your laptop), either can recover the other. Second: you designate a small group of trusted friends as guardians. If you lose every device, the guardians collectively approve your recovery from a fresh device — no central support line, no seed phrase to write down, no exchange to call.
Where does my donation money actually go?
+
Donations flow through the Freedom Ship Trust (a charitable trust) to FreedomShip Inc. (the operating company that builds and runs the fleet). 100% of FSC sales fund the mission — and that means more than steel. It covers ship construction and operations (charter / acquisition / purpose-built vessels, refits, kitchens, hospitals, schools, satellite internet, crew, navigation), the legal and compliance cost of operating the Trust, the advertising and outreach that grows the community, and the other operating expenses of running a fleet. What it never covers is founder paydays — no founder draws a salary, dividend, or payout from donations. FSC is not used to fund external humanitarian programs; the humanitarian dimension is the ships themselves — free lifelong cabins for the waiting list.
Is my donation tax-deductible?
+
Deductibility depends on your country of residence and the Trust's registration status in that jurisdiction. The Freedom Ship Trust is structured as a charitable trust; check with a tax adviser for whether your local rules apply. The app shows your donation history and receipt records inside your wallet for whatever documentation you need.
Can I send FSC to friends? Does it cost anything?
+
Yes — sending FSC peer-to-peer is fee-free and effectively instant. Holochain has no gas, no validator auction, and no global confirmation queue. You scan or paste a friend's identifier in the app, pick an amount, confirm with biometrics, and it lands in their wallet seconds later.
Where can I see the source code?
+
Every client application — desktop, mobile, and the PWA, including the FSC wallet and all on-device cryptography — is fully open source and publicly auditable. Anyone can read, build, and verify the exact code that runs on their own device. The only part kept private is the Treasury Service backend — the financial plumbing that handles fiat donations, banking rails, KYC/AML, and the price-and-reserve algorithm — which stays closed source for security and regulatory-compliance reasons. In short: everything that touches your keys and your coins is open; the bank-facing money systems are not. Release binaries and SHA-256 checksums are published at the freedomship-releases repo.
